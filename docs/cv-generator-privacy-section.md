## CV Generator

### Who is responsible

ArbeidMatch Norge AS, org.nr. 935 667 089, Sverre Svendsens veg 38, 7056 Ranheim, Norway,
is the controller for the personal data described in this section. You can reach us at
post@arbeidmatch.no.

### What the CV generator does

The CV generator at arbeidmatch.no/cv-gen is a free tool that builds a CV as a PDF file.
While you are filling in the form, the information stays in your own browser. It is not
sent to us, not stored on our servers, and not included in any analytics.

Your data reaches us only at the moment you ask to download the PDF and confirm two
things: that you accept this privacy policy and our terms of use, and that you agree we
create a work profile for you. We confirm that agreement with a six digit code sent to
your email address. If you do not complete that step, nothing is stored.

### Why we process your data, and on what basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, for
these purposes:

- to generate and send you your CV and cover letter as PDF files
- to create a work profile so we can match you with jobs we recruit for
- to send you relevant job openings by email, only if you asked us to

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing it does not affect the lawfulness
of processing carried out before you withdrew it.

### What we collect

The information you enter into the CV: your name, job title, email address, phone number,
city and country, work permit status, driving licence categories, professional summary,
work history, education, certificates, skills, languages and, if you write one, your cover
letter. We also store a record of your consent, which includes a hash of your email
address, a hash of your IP address, your browser's user agent string, the version of the
text you agreed to, and the time your code was verified.

### What we never ask for

We do not collect your national identity number (fodselsnummer, CNP or equivalent), your
date of birth, your marital status, your photograph, your nationality beyond your work
permit status, or any health information. Please do not enter any of it in the free text
fields.

### Who receives your data

- Our own recruitment system, so our consultants can match you with open roles.
- RecMan, the recruitment platform we use, acting as our data processor.
- Our email provider, to deliver your CV and the verification codes.
- Our hosting and database providers, to run the service.

Your CV is sent to a specific employer only after a separate, explicit action. It does not
happen automatically because you used the CV generator.

### Where your data is held

Within the EU and the EEA.

### How long we keep it

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Verification codes | 24 hours |
| Download and access links | 7 days after they expire |
| Your CV and cover letter files | 24 months |
| Your work profile | until you delete it |
| The record that you gave consent | kept after erasure, with your identity removed, as proof of the basis on which we held your data |

### Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object
to processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent.

The email containing your CV includes a link to a page where you can see everything we
hold, download it as a file, and delete all of it yourself. Deleting removes your work
profile and your documents, including the PDF files in our storage.

You also have the right to complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection
Authority.

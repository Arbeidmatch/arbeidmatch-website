## Photograph review before publication

Before publishing any new or replacement photograph, inspect the source at full
resolution and its final desktop/mobile crop. Trace each visible arm from shoulder
to elbow, wrist and hand; inspect fingers, legs, tool grip/contact, background
people, clothing logos and watermark text. A successful build or an image loading
in the browser does not prove visual correctness. Reject ambiguous anatomy.
Record the exact reviewed asset and findings in docs/image-review.md. Do not reuse
the rejected carpenter-2-workwear-v2.png or the removed tradesperson.webp: they
contain an extra arm. See the review for the replacement and source-library ID.
